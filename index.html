About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST) under Prof. Peter Wonka.

Prior to this, I obtained my M.S. degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from KAUST, and my B.Eng. degree in Electronic Information Engineering from Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT), China.

My research interests include 3D Vision and Perception (Depth Estimation / Surface Normal Estimation).

Outside of research, I am an enthusiast of 🏀 basketball, 🏃 running, and 🥏 ultimate frisbee.

Wenqing Cui, Zhenyu Li†, Mykola Lavreniuk†, Jian Shi, Ramzi Idoughi, Xiangjun Tang, Peter Wonka

IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR)

arXiv preprint arXiv:2601.22231

A study revisiting positional embeddings in vision transformers from a geometric perspective, revealing their role as geometric priors for spatial reasoning.

SIGGRAPH/TOG 2026
